Full Story: The Lion and the MouseA fierce lion was sleeping peacefully in the forest.
A tiny mouse accidentally ran across the lion's nose and woke him up.
 Furious, the giant lion pinned the mouse under his huge paw and opened his jaw to swallow him.
 Trembling with fear, the little mouse pleaded,
  "Please spare my life! If you let me go,
  I will always remember your kindness and help you one day.
  "The lion laughed at the idea that such a tiny creature could ever help the king of the jungle. Still, he felt generous and lifted his paw, letting the mouse scurry away.A few weeks later, the lion wandered into the forest and stumbled into a hidden hunter's net. He struggled with all his might, but the thick ropes held him fast. Desperate, his roars echoed through the trees.The little mouse recognized the roar and raced to the rescue. Working quickly with his sharp teeth, he gnawed through the heavy ropes until the net snapped apart. The lion crawled out of the trap, free at last. From that day on, the mighty lion and the tiny mouse were best friends.
Moral: Kindness, no matter how small, is never wasted.
